## Hello, I'm alphapixelguild 👋

I write Rust and C for things that live close to the metal — small terminal tools, allocators, and the occasional unsafe block I promise to revisit. When the benchmark numbers look smug, I compile whatever I can to WebAssembly and go stare at a flamegraph until they stop being smug.
